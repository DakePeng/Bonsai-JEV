Bonsai-JEV

A 27B-class calibrated decision model that fits on an 8 GB GPU — text and images, one forward pass.

The JEV recipe (LoRA + 24-slot decision head, distilled to soft labels) trained on Ternary Bonsai 2 27B, Prism ML's ternary (−1/0/+1) Qwen3.8-27B. The base stays ternary and frozen; the adapter is a 0.23–0.46 GB file. All numbers measured through the deployment path (PrismML llama.cpp fork).
